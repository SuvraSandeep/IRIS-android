# Continuous voice — 10.2.0 (339)

The previous pipeline waited for utterance silence, refused all media playback and reopened capture after a spoken greeting. This release uses one continuous microphone for wake and offline commands, with a bounded eight-second RAM buffer and an explicit acoustic wake boundary. A dedicated streaming subsequence DTW detector reuses the same log-mel frontend as enrollment and proposes candidates inside ongoing speech. It is a custom recorded-sound streaming detector, not an openWakeWord neural model or a claim of pretraining on thousands of examples. The candidate must still pass saved phrase evidence, negatives, owner identity, route, model fingerprint and current revision. There is no automatic strictness reduction or self-training.

Fresh enrollment uses bundled SpeechBrain ECAPA alongside valid Vosk identity. The export is pinned to an upstream checkpoint revision; a real-valued DFT frontend avoids unsupported complex STFT export. CI must compare ONNX with PyTorch at 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 seconds before bundling. The app verifies a SHA-256 manifest before loading. Existing Vosk-only profiles keep their evidence; fresh authenticated training is needed for the new identity model. A candidate with insufficient identity can gather up to two seconds of following context and re-check at unchanged strictness. Command PCM remains buffered while verification runs.

Music no longer categorically disables wake. Own replies are excluded from wake by explicit state. Replay/loudspeaker rejection is not guaranteed; owner voice plus phrase recognition cannot prove liveness. Bluetooth capture may still change headset audio quality depending on Android/hardware. Foreground-service microphone priority restrictions still apply. Android silencing, route changes, actual PCM readiness, command overruns and microphone handoffs are logged. Stale callbacks are generation-guarded; native models wait for decoder leases before release. External Android/system and server speech retain their explicit settings and require their own microphone handoff; seamless buffered capture applies to the offline path.

Settings: optional local reliability study, separate consent, encrypted metadata only, bounded event history, manual attempt labels, measured wake-audio exposure, first-call success and labelled unwanted wakes/hour. No raw audio/transcripts persisted by the study and no automatic threshold changes. Study accuracy requires users to label all deliberate attempts and unwanted wakes, ideally 50–100 calls across phone/headset, noise, distance, and screen off. User measurement cannot be generated in CI.

Validation: scripts/test-speech.sh; Android unit tests; verified model export; APK build. New tests exercise phrase-in-continuous-speech candidates, wrong-stream rejection, buffered command boundaries, overrun/erasure and dual-model identity requirements. These are synthetic/pipeline checks, not phone/headset accuracy or battery benchmarks.
